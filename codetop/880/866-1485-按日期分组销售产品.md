## 最佳解法思路
- **按日期分组 + 去重拼接**  
对每个 `sell_date`：  
  - `COUNT(DISTINCT product)` 统计不同产品数  
  - `GROUP_CONCAT(DISTINCT product ORDER BY product)` 输出按字典序拼接的产品列表

## SQL 最佳实现

```sql
SELECT
  sell_date,
  COUNT(DISTINCT product) AS num_sold,
  GROUP_CONCAT(DISTINCT product ORDER BY product SEPARATOR ',') AS products
FROM Activities
GROUP BY sell_date
ORDER BY sell_date;
```
